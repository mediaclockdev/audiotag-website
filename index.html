How it works

Shoot it. Say it. Send it.

Speak the name

Hold the mic and describe the shot. Audiotag writes the filename, so you never type on a ladder or in the rain.

Nothing gets lost

Photos stay in date-taken order and the original metadata is never overwritten.

Notes become reports

Dictate the full observation on each photo, then export the job as a PDF with images and notes side by side.

Import

Your whole day, grouped by the day.

Audiotag reads straight from the camera roll and stacks the shots under the date they were taken — today, yesterday, last Tuesday's job. Tap the ones you need and start.

  • Select all, or jump to 25 or 50 at a time
  • Time stamp shown on every thumbnail

Reports

From the driveway to the client's inbox.

Every named photo carries its spoken note with it. Pick the job, and Audiotag builds the PDF in order — no re-typing, no copy-paste into a template on Monday morning.
